Title-Case section headline with the brand's two colour-emphasis patterns built in.

```jsx
<SectionHeading trail="impact in education, research, and innovation">
  Our numbers reflect a tradition of excellence and forward-thinking
</SectionHeading>
<SectionHeading accent="Academic Programs for Every Ambition">A World-Class Range of</SectionHeading>
```

`trail` = grey continuation; `accent` = gold clause. Never use both on one heading.
